# Examined Human Budget Form

> [!info] Using this template
> A budget may span a week, month, season, year, or any other period of at least four calendar days. Dates use `YYYY-MM-DD`. Budget periods cannot overlap. Reimporting the exact same start and end dates replaces that stored budget; revisions are not retained.

##### Budget Targets

FORMAT:
`unit | signed amount | engagement`

EXAMPLES (do not copy these below `ENTRIES:` unless they are real):
`USD | -300 | Nutrition and Dietary Practices`
`USD | -120 | My Online Gear`
`TOMAN | -20000000 | Maintenance - Home`
`USD | 1000 | Freelance Work`

Negative targets are expected spending or liabilities; positive targets are expected income or assets. Use an existing canonical engagement name or alias.

##### Expected Movements

FORMAT:
`date | unit | signed amount | account | engagement | description`

EXAMPLES (do not copy these below `ENTRIES:` unless they are real):
`2026-09-05 | USD | -40 | Cash | Nutrition and Dietary Practices | groceries`
`2026-09-10 | USD | 1000 | Bank | Freelance Work | expected invoice payment`

The date must fall inside this budget period. Use an existing account and engagement name or alias. The unit must match the account's configured unit. Expected movements are plans only: they do not create transactions or reminders.
